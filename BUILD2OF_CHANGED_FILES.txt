HLAVNI ZMENY:
- navazuje na BUILD2OE
- zvuk z BUILD2OD/2OE zustava: playback 48 kHz + hard reset mezi hrami
- opravena hlavni chyba BUILD2OE: region config se nepsal, protoze FS jeste neexistoval
- BUILD2OF zapisuje configuration.ini v Module.preRun, kdy uz Emscripten FS existuje a jeste pred startem C++ frontendu
- cesta zustava oficialni ClownMDEmu Emscripten persistent path: /libsdl/clownacy/clownmdemu-frontend/configuration.ini
- region se porad voli automaticky podle nazvu ROM:
  * Europe/EUR/PAL/SECAM/(E) -> PAL_EU: pal=on, japanese=off
  * USA/US/Genesis/(U) -> NTSC_US: pal=off, japanese=off
  * Japan/Japanese/(J) -> NTSC_JP: pal=off, japanese=on
- pridany logy REGION_PRERUN_START / REGION_CONFIG_WRITTEN / REGION_PRERUN_DONE
