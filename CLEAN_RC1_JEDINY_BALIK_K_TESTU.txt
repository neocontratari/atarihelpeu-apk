- Whole-phone mirror pres Android MediaProjection, aby TV WEB CAST videl i obraz mimo AtariHelp, vcetne YouTube obrazu a systemovych obrazovek.
- App-only fallback, kdyz uzivatel nepovoli sdileni cele obrazovky.
- PS1/Sega audio do TV WEB CAST.

- MP3/WAV player audio do TV WEB CAST az za EQ/bass/treble/balance/volume.
- MP3/WAV vnitrni knihovnu v playeru.
