AtariHelp.eu EMU-10 BUILD2CG_BY_BZ_GOLDEN_CLOAD_RECORD132_RAW_FIX

KODY JSOU STEJNE.
Zaklad: BUILD2BZ/BY golden.
TXT line queue z BUILD2BZ NEMENEN.
Joystick/scroll/focus z BUILD2BY NEMENEN.
CLOAD kratke golden vetve se nevraci k BV/BW/BX.

CO SE ZMENILO:
- Oprava pouze pro CLOAD vlastniho LAST CSAVE.
- Predchozi BR/BZ cesta preskocila prvni dve $55 u vlastniho CSAVE a zacinala SERIN az controlem $FC.
- Standardni Atari kazetovy record ma 132 B: $55, $55, control byte, 128 datovych B, checksum.
- BUILD2CG pousti do SERIN kompletni raw recordy 132 B vcetne $55,$55/control/checksum.
- Mezi recordy pridava kratky IRG mark tone cca 0.25 s.
- Android monitor zvuk kazety je zvednuty z 0.14 na 0.55, aby nebyl potichu.
- Zadny RAM inject, zadne cteni BASIC programu z RAM, zadne fake READY/CLOAD.

TEST:
1) POWER -> READY.
2) BASIC/TBXL TXT -> vlozit testovaci BASIC kod.
3) VLOZIT + RUN -> musi probehnout SUM OK a KONEC OK.
4) CSAVE -> cekat CSAVE VYSLEDEK: ANO.
5) LAST CSAVE / SAVE.
6) NEW, LIST musi byt prazdny.
7) CLOAD, potom PLAY.
8) Po navratu READY: LIST a RUN.
