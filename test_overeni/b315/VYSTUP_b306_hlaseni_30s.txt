po F5 jede Sega (~25 s), PS1 (~20 s), demo (~11 s), pak 33 s bez proudu a 3 s Sega. Kdyz 30s tik padne do prepnuti

strance. B315 pise hlaseni stejne (navic ", vystup ..."); deterministicky to overuje test_b315_zvuk_tv.py (30. a 60. s).
