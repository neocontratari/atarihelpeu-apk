CHAPTER 01

MAIN MENU

CARDS AND CONTROLS

The main menu is a row of big cards – one card, one world. The order: ATARI 130XE SEGA MEGA DRIVE PS1 MP3 PLAYER JUKEBOX ATARIHELP.EU HELP. The menu is drawn by pure C++ straight on the graphics chip, so it runs smoothly with no delay.

The first start

  • When Sonic the Hedgehog (Sega) or the PlayStation BIOS is missing on the phone, the app first asks whether to download them from atarihelp.eu: DOWNLOAD / NOT NOW. The app works without them too.
  • Then the intro film plays. Skip it with the SKIP › button or the phone's BACK button. Play it again in OPTIONS → PLAY INTRO.
  • Finally the main menu appears – the cards fly into place.
  • The app speaks the language of the phone (Czech or English). You can switch it any time: OPTIONS → JAZYK · LANGUAGE.

Browsing and opening

Drag
The cards follow your finger smoothly left and right. Let go – the card glides to the middle.
Flick
A quick flick skips several cards at once. A finger on the moving cards stops them.
Tap the middle
Opens the middle card (the card lights up and the app switches).
Button under the card
START, PLAY, LET'S GO, OPEN WEBSITE, OPEN GUIDE – opens the middle card.
Card next to it
Tap it and it slides to the middle.
Dots
Tap a dot to jump straight to that card.
Gear ⚙
Top right – opens OPTIONS.

Keyboard, controller, remote control

  • ← → (also L1 / R1 on a controller) – browse the cards.
  • Enter, OK, A or the space bar – opens the middle card.
  • Home / End – the first / last card.
  • On a TV or PC via WEB TV you browse with the arrow keys (or A / D) and open with Enter – see WEB TV and PC.

Locked cards

The SEGA MEGA DRIVE and PS1 cards unlock step by step. A locked card is grey with a padlock and the word LOCKED. When you tap it, it shakes and the text under the cards shows what is still missing (for example how many program lines to type). How: Unlocking.

Back to the menu

  • Every section has a MENU button (in PS1 BACK, on the DJ console MENU / ⏻).
  • Or the phone's BACK button.
  • The BACK button in the main menu itself sends the app to the background – when you come back, the menu is waiting for you.

CHAPTER 02

ATARI 130XE

EMULATOR · C++

An Atari 130XE with 128 kB of memory and the VBXE graphics card, written entirely in C++. The whole machine is on the display: the screen, the console buttons, the keyboard, the tape deck and, at the very bottom, the service buttons.

Power and console

POWER
The green switch on the left by the screen – switches the Atari off / on. Switching on is a cold start: the memory is cleared.
RESET
Like on a real Atari – the program in memory stays.
HELP, START, SELECT, OPTION
Console buttons above the keyboard, held for as long as your finger is on them. (HELP here is the Atari's HELP key.)
SHIFT, CONTROL
A tap = locked (gold), another tap = unlocked.

Joystick

In portrait – the joystick is the Atari screen

  • Put a finger on the left half of the screen and move it (up, down, sideways, diagonals too) – the joystick points that way for as long as you hold the finger.
  • The right half of the screen = FIRE (held while the finger is down). Both thumbs work at once.

In landscape – turn the phone

  • The Atari picture fills the whole display. The D-pad is bottom left (the dot follows your thumb, diagonals too), FIRE, SPACE and RETURN on the right, START / SELECT / OPTION top left.
  • The gear ⚙ at the top right = D-PAD AND CONTROLS: EDIT BUTTON LAYOUT (drag with a finger, IN GROUPS, DEFAULT, DONE), CONTROLS: SENSITIVITY AND LOOK (sensitivity, transparency, button and D-pad size, vibration), SWAP D-PAD AND ACTIONS (LEFT-HANDED), DEFAULT BUTTON LAYOUT, RESET ATARI, games, disk, tape and help. The app remembers your settings.

Game controller (Bluetooth / USB)

D-pad or stick = joystick, A / B = FIRE, X = SPACE, Y = RETURN, START / SELECT = console, L2 / R2 = OPTION.

Service buttons (at the very bottom)

NET / GAMES
Games from atarihelp.eu – Galactic Chase, Moon Patrol, Pac-Man, Donkey Kong, River Raid, Bruce Lee, Montezuma's Revenge, René's Pitt-Kitt and more. Tap a game, it downloads and starts. The WEB button opens the games website.
XEX / PHONE
Starts a game from the phone – a XEX file or a CAR cartridge (both also packed in a ZIP). The cartridge goes into the slot and the Atari powers on with it (it covers BASIC, like a real cartridge). It stays in the slot even after switching off and on, another program (XEX, ATR, TXT) takes it out. When a cartridge is in the slot, it offers REMOVE THE CARTRIDGE (the Atari restarts with BASIC) and CHOOSE ANOTHER FILE.
ATR / DISK
An ATR disk (also in a ZIP) into drive D1: and boot from the disk (OPTION held = BASIC off). The disk stays in the drive even after switching off and on – like a real drive. When there is a disk in D1:, it offers EJECT THE DISK (the Atari restarts with BASIC), SWAP THE DISK WITHOUT RESTART (the game wants the next disk or the other side) and INSERT ANOTHER DISK AND BOOT.
TURBO / BASIC
Loads Turbo-BASIC XL 1.5.
BASIC/TBXL TXT
A program from a text file: TXT FILE → ATARI BASIC or TXT FILE → TURBO-BASIC XL – the app types the program itself on the Atari keyboard, line by line (then RUN, CSAVE…). Or TYPE / PASTE TEXT MANUALLY and PASTE / PASTE + RUN.
LOG / ERROR
Log and tests – see Log and errors.
HELP
A short controls help right in the Atari.
MENU
Back to the main menu.

Tape deck

  • REC and PLAY stay down, STOP releases them. REW = tape to the start, FWD = 10 s further. The small button next to the counter resets it.
  • EJECT opens the door and offers tapes – CSAVE recordings or any WAV from the phone. Once you pick one, the tape goes in and the door closes.

Saving a program to tape (CSAVE)

  1. Type CSAVE and RETURN.
  2. After the beep, RETURN again.
  3. After recording, the WAV is saved by itself to Download/AtariHelp/Atari_emu/csave_<date>_<time>.wav – it is the exact signal for a real tape recorder.

Loading a program from tape (CLOAD)

  1. EJECT and pick a tape (PLAY is pressed by itself).
  2. Type CLOAD and RETURN – the Atari beeps and waits.
  3. Press RETURN once more. The motor starts, the program loads, then READY and you can LIST or RUN.

While loading you hear what a real Atari plays (POKE 65,0 = silent). With a stereo WAV also the tape's audio track.

GAMES ON TAPE The app recognises a tape that starts by itself (boot): the Atari powers on with START + OPTION held, presses RETURN after the beep and the game loads. Manually: hold START and OPTION and flip POWER. In landscape you find the tapes in the gear ⚙ → TAPE (WAV).

VBXE graphics

The VBXE graphics card is built into the Atari – VBXE games (Wolfenstein 3D, Popeye…) find it by themselves. Wolfenstein 3D: walk with the joystick, FIRE = shoot, SPACE = open doors.

Typing programs

Type on the machine's keyboard or on a computer keyboard via WEB TV. Every 50 lines you type yourself unlock the Sega – see Unlocking.

MY GAMES – games saved on the phone. Tap a game to start it, the bin deletes it (JUST THE GAME (the saved position stays) or THE GAME AND THE SAVED POSITION). PICK A FILE ON THE PHONE MANUALLY, REFRESH THE LIST.
CARTRIDGE
The slot on the console – choose a ROM from the phone (.gen, .bin, .md, .smd, .zip).
SAVES
SAVED POSITIONS – games with a saved position. Tap one to load the position.
MANUALS

WEB
Opens atarihelp.eu.
MENU

PS1

PLAYSTATION · 32-BIT

32-bit PlayStation games with an on-screen controller. The card unlocks after 30 minutes of playing the Sega – Unlocking.

Buttons

LOAD GAME
Opens the PS1 page on atarihelp.eu with Google Drive links. Tap a game – the first time it downloads and unpacks, next time it starts straight from the phone.
CD ISO
The library CHOOSE A GAME (CD/ISO) – games on the phone, PICK A FILE ON THE PHONE MANUALLY, deleting games.
SAVE GAME
Saves the exact game position.
MEMORY CARD
MEMORY CARD – the state of the memory card. Games save to it by themselves (like to a real card).
BACK
Back to the main menu.

Controller: D-pad, △ ○ ✕ □, L1 L2 R1 R2, SELECT, START and RESET.

Gear ⚙ – D-PAD AND CONTROLS

EDIT BUTTON LAYOUT, CONTROLS: SENSITIVITY AND LOOK, SWAP D-PAD AND ACTIONS (LEFT-HANDED), LOAD QUICK POSITION (loads the position from SAVE GAME – exactly where you left off), SAVE AND SEND THE LOG, PLAYSTATION MOUSE (DUNE 2000 …) and MORE (BIOS, CD, LOG...).

Games and BIOS

  • Game formats: .bin .cue .iso .img .pbp .chd and .zip. The app cannot open .7z.
  • PS1 needs a BIOS (the file scph….bin). When it is missing, the app takes it from the Download/AtariHelp folder (also from the BIOS or PS1_BIOS subfolder, or from a ZIP with “bios” in its name), otherwise it downloads it from atarihelp.eu. Manually: OPTIONS → DOWNLOAD GAMES AND BIOS.

TIP On a TV / PC: arrows or WASD direction, K or space = ✕, L = ○, J = □, I = △, Q / E = L1 / R1, Z / C = L2 / R2, Enter or P = START, Backspace or O = SELECT.

PlayStation mouse (Dune 2000 …)

Games that support the PlayStation mouse (Dune 2000, Command & Conquer, Warzone 2100 …) are played in landscape with your finger right on the picture – the game picture is a touchpad like on a laptop. Switch it on with the MOUSE button at the top left, settings: gear ⚙ → PLAYSTATION MOUSE (DUNE 2000 …).

  • Drag your finger over the picture = cursor movement. The cursor starts moving smoothly once your finger has travelled a little – so taps stay precise. Tap = left button, tap with two fingers = right.
  • Acceleration like a computer mouse: a slow drag = fine precise movement, a fast drag = the cursor travels further. Switch it off in the mouse settings with the ACCELERATION: ON button.
  • Hold your finger still until it vibrates (HOLDING THE LEFT BUTTON shows at the top) and drag = select units with a box.
  • Mouse in port 1: ✕ = left and ○ = right mouse button (you can hold them too), D-pad = slow precise cursor movement. A game with only the mouse does not see the other buttons, so they are hidden.
  • Games look for the mouse in different places: MOUSE IN PORT 1 (DUNE 2000), MOUSE PORT 1 + CONTROLLER PORT 2 or CONTROLLER PORT 1 + MOUSE PORT 2. The cursor speed can be changed.
  • The choice is remembered for each game separately – next time the mouse is plugged in right from power-on.

TIP On a TV / PC through WEB TV: click into the picture – the computer mouse then moves the game cursor, its left and right buttons are the PlayStation mouse buttons, Esc releases the mouse. With the mouse in port 1 the keyboard works too: arrows = cursor, K or space = left, L = right mouse button.

CHAPTER 05

MP3 PLAYER

RETRO CASSETTE PLAYER

A retro cassette deck for music from your phone – MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG and more. Equalizer, bass, treble, playlists, favorite tracks and recording your own mixtape.

Playing

PLAY
Plays a track. When the list is empty, the playlist and the music picker open.
PAUSE / STOP
Pauses / stops.
REWIND / FAST FWD
10 seconds back / forward.
RECORD
Records your own mixtape – see below.

When a track ends, the next one plays.

Playlist

MY PLAYLISTS, PLAYLISTS, LIBRARY or the track strip open the panel with the music list on the phone PHONE - LOCAL MUSIC (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG...):

  • Tapping a track plays it (and adds it to the playlist). The star ☆ next to it adds it to the favorites.
  • REFRESH THE PHONE LIST – finds the music on the phone (Downloads, Music, Documents…), including recordings from RECORD and from the JUKEBOX.
  • PICK WITH THE SYSTEM – you pick the files yourself.
  • CLEAR THE PLAYLIST, CLOSE. The phone's BACK button closes an open panel too.

FAVORITES – favorite tracks

  • The star ☆ next to a track (in the playlist and in the phone's music list) adds it to the favorites – it turns into ★. Another tap removes it again.
  • FAVORITES at the bottom opens the favorites: tapping a track plays it, ✕ removes it (first it asks REMOVE?, tap again).
  • ★ ADD THE PLAYING TRACK / ☆ REMOVE THE PLAYING TRACK – the track playing right now into the favorites / out.
  • ▶ PLAY ALL FAVORITES – a playlist of the favorites, playing one after another.
  • The app remembers the favorites even after it is closed. When you move a file on the phone, it finds it by name; when it is no longer on the phone, it says TRACK NOT AVAILABLE.
  • It works with tracks from the phone – not with a file picked in a browser on the computer.

RECORD – your own mixtape

  • RECORD records whatever the player plays – including the EQ, BASS, TREBLE and BALANCE settings. The VOLUME knob does not make the recording quieter.
  • When nothing is playing, playback starts by itself – like on a tape deck. The button glows softly red and the time ● REC runs at the bottom.
  • PAUSE pauses recording (❚❚ REC), PLAY continues. Neither the pause nor loading the next track is recorded – the tracks follow each other directly in the recording.
  • Ending and saving: RECORD again, STOP, the end of the playlist, MENU or the phone's BACK button (the app saves first, then it leaves). At most 90 minutes – like a C90 cassette.
  • The recording is saved to Download/AtariHelp/Mixtape as NP_VISION_mixtape_<date>_<time>.m4a (on an older phone .webm) and MIXTAPE SAVED appears at the bottom. It is in the phone's music list right away.

A MIXTAPE FROM THE FAVORITES FAVORITES → ▶ PLAY ALL FAVORITES, then STOP and RECORD. The whole playlist is recorded and saved by itself at the end.

Sound

  • EQ 60/250/1K/4K/16K – five bands ±12 dB, drag up / down with a finger.
  • BALANCE – drag sideways. BASS / TREBLE / VOLUME – bass and treble ±12 dB, volume.
  • SETTINGS → RESET SOUND TO DEFAULT returns the sound to its default state.

More

  • YOUTUBE – opens YouTube right in the app.
  • MENU – back to the main menu.
  • While WEB TV is running, the music plays on the TV / computer too.

JUKEBOX

PRO DJ MIXER

Two decks, a crossfader, equalizers and samples. Big bass that gets everyone dancing! A short help is right on the console too, under the HELP button.

Loading tracks

USB 1 ▾ (deck A) and USB 2 ▾ (deck B) – or PLAYLIST, BROWSE, LIBRARY – open the deck's playlist: + ADD adds tracks from the phone, ◇ LOAD loads the selected one into the deck.

Deck

CUE
Jumps to the start and plays.
▶ PLAY / ❚❚
Play / pause.
STUTTER
A stutter 0.15 s back.
◀◀ / ▶▶
5 seconds back / forward.
SYNC
Matches the tempo to the other deck.
TAP
Tap out the tempo (BPM) – at least 2 taps.
KEY LOCK
The tempo changes, the key stays.
TEMPO, GAIN
Tempo ±8 %, deck volume.
EQ
10 bands (31 Hz – 16 kHz) under the faders.
HOT CUE
Pads: the 1st tap saves a point, the next tap jumps to it, holding deletes it.

Mixing and samples

  • CROSSFADE – smoothly from deck A to B. ◄ HARD A / HARD B ► – instantly just one deck. ⇄ SYNC BPM matches the tempos.
  • MASTER: BAL, BASS, TREB; MASTER LIMITER ON protects against clipping.
  • Sampler: KICK SNARE HAT CLAP BASS SYNTH VOCAL FX.
  • RECORD records the whole mix, a second tap saves the recording to Download/AtariHelp/DJ_mixy (M4A, WebM on an older phone). When you leave the console while recording (MENU / ⏻ or the phone's BACK button), the mix is saved first.
  • EFFECTS at the top hides / shows the EQ rows, SETTINGS shows the assistant, MENU / ⏻ = back to the app.

CHAPTER 09

WEB TV AND PC

PICTURE AND SOUND IN A BROWSER

The app can send both picture and sound to an ordinary browser on a TV or computer – no cable and nothing to install. On a PC you can then play and type with the keyboard.

How to switch it on

  1. The phone and the TV / PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. In the main menu, the gear ⚙ → START WEB TV. An address appears under the button, for example http://192.168.1.23:8765/ – the numbers depend on your network. You can find the address again any time: ⚙ → under WEB TV IS RUNNING.
  3. On the TV or PC open a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox…) and type that address exactly, including the number after the colon.
  4. Sound: the browser plays sound only after a click or a key press – click anywhere in the picture (again after reloading the page).

WEB TV runs until you close the app. While streaming, the phone keeps the Wi-Fi in low-latency mode.

In the browser

  • Full screen on a PC: the F11 key.
  • Move the mouse (or touch the picture) – the PICTURE panel appears at the bottom right: brightness, contrast, saturation, ORIGINAL SETTINGS, PICTURE: fill the screen and picture tuning. The browser remembers the settings.
  • At the bottom right there is always a hint which keys work right now.
  • PS1 with the PlayStation mouse switched on: click into the picture – the computer mouse moves the game cursor (movement and both buttons), Esc releases it.

Computer keyboard

MAIN MENU

← → or A D browse, Enter / space opens a card, Home / End first / last.

ATARI – TYPING

Type normally (a Czech keyboard works too). F1 START, F3 SELECT, F4 OPTION, F2 BREAK, F6 HELP, F8 INVERSE, Ctrl + key = CONTROL. F9 switches to game mode.

ATARI – GAME MODE (F9)

WASD / arrows = joystick, L = FIRE, K / space = jump, F1 START. F9 back to typing.

SEGA

WASD move, K = A, L = B, O = C, P = START.

PS1

Arrows / WASD, K / space ✕, L ○, J □, I △, Q / E L1 / R1, Z / C L2 / R2, Enter / P START, Backspace / O SELECT.

A PROGRAM FROM THE PC INTO THE ATARI When the Atari 130XE is on the TV: Ctrl + V pastes text from the clipboard, or drag a TXT file into the browser window – the app types it into the Atari (512 kB at most).

CHAPTER 10

UNLOCKING

SEGA AND PS1

From the start the Atari 130XE, the MP3 player, the JUKEBOX, ATARIHELP.EU and HELP are open. The Sega and the PlayStation unlock step by step – like a retro gamer's journey from 8-bit onwards.

SEGA MEGA DRIVE – type 50 lines on the Atari

  • Program lines count – numbered lines, for example 10 PRINT "HELLO".
  • They are counted when you RUN or LIST.
  • At least 5 lines must be added at a time – a shorter batch does not count. It does not have to be one program: ten programs of five lines each will do.
  • Only what you type yourself counts – on the machine's keyboard or on a computer keyboard via WEB TV. Pasted text (Ctrl + V, a dragged file, BASIC/TBXL TXT) does not count.
  • When it is done, the Atari shows SEGA UNLOCKED! IT IS IN THE MENU.

PS1 – play the Sega for 30 minutes

  • Only the time when a game is really running on the Sega counts – not the app in the background or another emulator.
  • The minutes add up, you do not have to play in one go.

The locks apply everywhere

A locked console is not opened even by a game from the atarihelp.eu website (a link, a downloaded file, a Google Drive link) – the app does not download it, does not start it and says why, for example SEGA IS LOCKED: “Type 37 more program lines on the Atari…” Likewise the emulator page does not open any other way – the app returns to the main menu.

HOW MUCH MORE? Tap the locked card in the menu – the text under the cards shows how many lines or minutes are still missing.

CHAPTER 12

LOG AND ERROR REPORTS

WHEN SOMETHING FAILS

The app writes down what it does (the log). When something fails, send the log – it is the fastest way to find the bug.

Atari 130XE

  1. On the machine tap LOG / ERROR.
  2. Press SAVE AND SEND THE LOG – the log is saved as a file and a menu opens right away to choose how to send it (mail, Messenger…). To be safe it is also on the clipboard.
  3. Back to the Atari: BACK TO THE ATARI 130XE or BACK.

WHEN THE APP CRASHED On the LOG / ERROR page, next to Previous file (after a crash) there is the path to the log of the previous run – send that file.

PS1 and Sega

  • PS1: the gear ⚙ → SAVE AND SEND THE LOG – the log is saved to Download/AtariHelp and a menu opens right away to choose how to send it.
  • Sega: in landscape the gear ⚙ → EXPORT LOG (DIAGNOSTICS) – the log is saved to Download/AtariHelp.

CHAPTER 13

FAQ

QUICK ANSWERS

The SEGA or PS1 card is locked. What now?

The Sega unlocks after 50 typed program lines on the Atari, the PS1 after 30 minutes of playing the Sega. Tap the locked card to see how much is still missing. In detail: Unlocking.

A game from the web did not start, the app says the Sega / PS1 is locked.

The locks apply to games from atarihelp.eu too. Once you unlock the console, the same game from the web starts. In detail: Unlocking.

How do I record my own mixtape?

In the MP3 player play some tracks (for example ▶ PLAY ALL FAVORITES) and press RECORD. The recording is saved to Download/AtariHelp/Mixtape. In detail: MP3 player.

There is no sound on the TV.

Click anywhere in the picture in the browser (or press a key). Browsers play sound only after a click – and again after reloading the page.

The WEB TV address does not open in the browser.

The phone and the TV / PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. Type the address exactly, including the number after the colon (for example :8765). Some networks (especially guest Wi-Fi) do not let devices talk to each other – try your home network.

The picture on the TV stutters or the sound crackles.

In OPTIONS switch to MANUAL and try a lower PICTURE QUALITY (WEB TV) and LOW for the sound (the most reliable). Moving the phone closer to the router helps too.

A PS1 game does not start.

Check the format: .bin, .cue, .iso, .img, .pbp, .chd or .zip (the app cannot open .7z). PS1 needs a BIOS – the app downloads it by itself, or OPTIONS → DOWNLOAD GAMES AND BIOS. If it still does not work, send the log (Log and errors).

Where do I find my games, positions and tapes?

In the Download/AtariHelp folder – the overview is in the chapter Files on the phone.

Do I lose anything when I uninstall the app?

Games, saved positions, the PS1 memory card and Atari tapes stay in Download/AtariHelp. The unlocking progress (the Sega and PS1 will be locked again), the app settings and the chosen language are deleted.

The player does not find my songs.

When the app asks for access to music, allow it (if you refused, allow it in the phone's settings for the app). Then REFRESH THE PHONE LIST, or pick the files yourself via PICK WITH THE SYSTEM. The player plays MP3 and WAV.

How do I get a program from the computer into the Atari?

Switch on WEB TV, open the address on the PC, start the Atari 130XE and press Ctrl + V (or drag a TXT file into the browser). On the phone the same works via BASIC/TBXL TXT.

A game from NET / GAMES does not download.

Check the internet and try again in a moment. You can also start a game from a file on the phone via XEX / PHONE.

The on-screen controller does not suit me.

In landscape the gear ⚙ → D-PAD AND CONTROLS: drag the buttons wherever you like, set the sensitivity, size and transparency, and swap them for left-handers. It works for the Atari, Sega and PS1.

How do I update the app?

OPTIONS → UPDATE THE APP. The phone will still ask whether you allow the installation.

I want to see the intro film again.

OPTIONS → PLAY INTRO. Skip it with the SKIP › button or BACK.

How do I switch the app to Czech?

OPTIONS → JAZYK · LANGUAGE → ČEŠTINA. The whole app switches right away.
