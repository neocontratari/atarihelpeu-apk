Jak mi log poslat

1. Kdyz appka NESPADLA — zmackni ULOZIT LOG A ODESLAT. Log se ulozi jako soubor do telefonu a hned se otevre nabidka, cim ho poslat (mail, Messenger, cokoli). Nemusis nic hledat ani nikam vkladat. Pro jistotu je zaroven i ve schrance.

2. Kdyz appka SPADLA — otevri spravce souboru a jdi na cestu, ktera je vypsana vys u polozky Soubor z minula. Ten soubor mi posli.

3. Kdyz u Soubor teto relace svitilo 0 B — pak se log na disk neuklada a bod 2 nefunguje. Poznas to i v logu podle radku zapis_funguje=false. V tom pripade pouzij bod 1, ten funguje vzdy.

1. If the app did NOT crash — press SAVE AND SEND THE LOG. The log is saved as a file on the phone and a menu opens right away to choose how to send it (mail, Messenger, anything). Nothing to look for or paste anywhere. To be safe it is also copied to the clipboard.

2. If the app CRASHED — open a file manager and go to the path shown above next to Previous file. Send me that file.

3. If This session’s file showed 0 B — the log is not being saved to storage and step 2 will not work. You can also see it in the log by the line zapis_funguje=false. In that case use step 1, it always works.

B308: PS1 – DOOM WITH CEILINGS. The bug was in our PS1 core (the gpu_neon graphics chip at 2x resolution): Doom draws the floor and ceiling in rows 1 pixel high and the walls in columns 1 pixel wide – at 2x each such row was drawn only partly, so a quarter of the pixels were missing in the ceiling and floor (black stripes, jagged holes) and the walls had cracks. Now they are drawn in full; other games draw bit for bit the same as before.
