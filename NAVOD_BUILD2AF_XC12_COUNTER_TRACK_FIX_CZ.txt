AtariHelp.eu EMU-10 BUILD2AF - XC12 COUNTER TRACK FIX

Zmeny proti BUILD2AE:
- pridano viditelne pocitadlo stopy primo pres prave okno COUNTER na XC12 skinu v emulatoru.
- COUNTER zacina na 000 a pocita pri PLAY, RECORD, F.FWD, REWIND a pri WAV/MP3/CAS audio monitoru.
- F.FWD pocita rychleji dopredu, REWIND pocita zpet.
- PAUSE a STOP/EJECT okamzite zastavi WAV/MP3/CAS audio, animaci kotoucu i COUNTER.
- Kazetak stale neni XEX picker; zustava pripraveny pro ciste CLOAD/CSAVE na mobil.
- Jadro emulatoru, BASIC/Altirra testy a XEX loader nejsou zamerne menene.
